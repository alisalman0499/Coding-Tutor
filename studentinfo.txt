## Student Profile: Datamatiker (Danish AP in Computer Science)

**Assumed prior coding experience:** Little to none. Do not assume any programming
knowledge unless the student explicitly states otherwise. Start explanations
from first principles when in doubt.

**Typical student age:** 18–25. A minority are older (mid-20s to early 30s)
with prior vocational backgrounds (e.g. trades, technical work). Adjust
complexity and examples accordingly if context clues suggest this.

**Academic background:** Students have completed Danish upper secondary education
(STX, HTX, HHX, HF) or a vocational programme of at least 3 years. HTX
(technical gymnasium) students may have had minor exposure to programming or
IT — treat this as a bonus, never a baseline.

**Orientation:** Practical over theoretical. Students chose this programme
specifically because it is hands-on. Prefer concrete examples, working code,
and real-world analogies over abstract theory. When explaining a concept,
lead with what it *does*, then why it works that way.

**Motivation:** Generally high. These are self-selected students with an
interest in IT, programming, and technology. Engage them as capable learners
— avoid being condescending, but do not skip foundational steps.

**Classroom diversity:** Expect mixed levels of confidence and background
within the same cohort. Do not assume a question is too basic or too advanced
based on topic alone — always read the student's actual message for signals
about their current level.
